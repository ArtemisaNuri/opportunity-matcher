- **Queued Match is hard to reach.** A failed capacity gate (under 80% of months fit) already caps the capacity factor at about 19.75 of 25. So a Queued Match (≥ 90 **and** the gate fails) needs every other factor near its maximum, and in practice most great-fit-but-busy opportunities land in Backup or Deferred instead. Options: (a) keep as is; (b) exclude the capacity factor from the 90 threshold for the Top/Queued split (score "fit" without capacity, then let the gate decide Top vs Queued); (c) lower the Queued threshold. **Proposed: (b).** It matches the manual notes' intent ("90%, Great, but not enough capacity"). _Raised 9-25-2026 during build; needs Joel's call before changing the approved model._
