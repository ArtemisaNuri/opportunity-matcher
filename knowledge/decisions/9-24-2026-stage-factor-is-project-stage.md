# Decision · The 10-point stage factor scores project stage

- **Date:** 9-24-2026 · **Cycle:** [9-24-2026-opportunity-scoring-mvp](../cycles/9-24-2026-opportunity-scoring-mvp.md)
- **Status:** Active (graduated from `tentative-decisions.md`)

## Decision
The 10-point "stage" factor scores the **project stage**: Full Build / Prototype / Recovery. Points per stage are adjustable in Settings (defaults 10 / 9 / 6).

## Alternatives rejected
- A sales-pipeline stage (Lead → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation). It would need a new field and would score deal maturity rather than delivery fit.
